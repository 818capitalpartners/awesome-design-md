No. 818 is business-purpose only — non-owner-occupied investment real estate.

About $100K fix-and-flip · $150K DSCR. If it’s too small to deliver real value, we’ll say so.

Yes. Call (917) 993-9194 or email deals@818capitalpartners.com. Same path from intake to wire — Dealroom keeps the file visible either way.

No. We don’t sell borrower data. Only people on your file see your numbers.
